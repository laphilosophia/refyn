Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our community is dedicated to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors. By participating, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.

Standards

- Be respectful and considerate in your communications.
- Avoid demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior and speech.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and respond professionally.

Reporting

- To report unacceptable behavior, open an issue on this repository (you may mark it as private) or contact the maintainers directly via the repository's issue tracker.

Consequences

- Unacceptable behavior may be reported to project maintainers and can result in removal from the project at the maintainers' discretion.

For the full, canonical text, see: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
